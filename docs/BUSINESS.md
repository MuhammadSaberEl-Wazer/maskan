# Maskan — Business Reference

> **Purpose:** This is the canonical business reference for the Maskan project. It is intended to be readable by humans and AI coding/research agents (Codex, Claude Code, etc.) and to serve later as the source for an in-app HTML business explainer. Keep this file updated when a business decision changes.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Maskan (مسكن)** is an Egypt-wide managed-housing business serving both shared-housing residents and families renting complete apartments. Maskan is not intended to be a simple property-listing marketplace or a broker. The core proposition is to lease suitable apartments, prepare and furnish them to a consistent standard, then operate them as organized housing with clear contracts, transparent pricing, maintenance, cleaning, support, and resident rules.

Maskan's customer promise is **Speed, Trust, and Safety**. These three priorities should govern the product, operations, and all customer-facing communication:

1. **Speed:** make registration, discovery, booking, and move-in as simple and fast as possible.
2. **Trust:** show rent, deposits, inclusions, and any additional costs before commitment, and make one accountable Maskan team responsible for the resident experience.
3. **Safety:** verify and document identity, contracts, payments, deposits, maintenance, and operational actions so residents can live with confidence.

The current model has two distinct rental models: individual private rooms, shared rooms, and beds inside managed shared residences; and complete apartments rented to one family under one contract. A family-apartment price always represents the monthly price of the whole unit, never a per-person or per-bed price. Maskan is the operating company and the resident's contractual counterparty.

**Business category:** Managed Shared and Family Housing / Housing Operations.

**Current stage:** concept + market validation + Proof of Concept application. Any financial figures in the POC are illustrative demo assumptions, not actual company performance.

## 2. Problem

The target market contains substantial demand for flexible housing among students, employees, young professionals, people moving to or between Egyptian cities, Egyptians, Arabs, and foreigners. Much of the shared-housing supply is fragmented and informal.

Typical pain points Maskan aims to reduce include:

- unclear or inconsistent rental terms;
- dealing with individual landlords or informal intermediaries;
- uncertain maintenance responsibility;
- inconsistent furnishing and cleanliness;
- unclear utility costs;
- poor roommate compatibility;
- difficulty finding a reliable room or bed for medium-term stays;
- weak after-rental support;
- inconsistent house rules and deposit handling.

Maskan's thesis is that residents may value an organized operator rather than merely access to a property listing.

---

## 3. Maskan Solution

Maskan seeks suitable **unfurnished apartments**, leases them from owners, furnishes and prepares them, and rents the resulting inventory to residents.

Maskan can offer:

- private rooms;
- shared rooms;
- individual beds;
- complete apartments rented to one family;
- clearly defined monthly pricing;
- security deposits;
- formal Maskan contracts;
- furnished, move-in-ready accommodation;
- Wi-Fi and utilities according to product tier;
- scheduled cleaning according to product tier;
- maintenance and support;
- standardized house rules;
- digital resident services through the Maskan application.

Where technically and legally appropriate, large spaces may be subdivided using lightweight partitions. Partition quality must match the target product: economical solutions can be used for budget/student inventory, while higher tiers require stronger privacy and sound isolation.

The objective is **not** to maximize beds at the expense of habitability. Units must retain useful common space and should feel like managed homes, not overcrowded dormitories.

## 4. Target Customers

Maskan is intended to be broadly accessible, subject to legal requirements and unit-specific eligibility.

Potential residents include:

- university students;
- employees;
- young professionals;
- people relocating within or between Egyptian cities;
- Egyptians;
- Arab residents;
- foreign residents / expatriates.
- families seeking a complete managed apartment.

A valid identity document is required. The concept does not depend on unnecessarily restrictive occupation or age segmentation beyond legal and operational requirements.

### Shared-housing separation

- Male and female shared units are strictly separate.
- Families are not housed inside shared units.
- A family apartment is a separate inventory and contract type, occupied by one family and never mixed with unrelated shared-housing residents.

## 5. Product Tiers

Maskan uses one master brand with multiple operating standards.

### 5.1 Maskan Essential

Target: students and price-sensitive residents.

- shared rooms are common;
- practical, durable furnishing;
- lightweight/economic partitions can be acceptable when safe and legally permitted;
- lower price point;
- services may use fair-use policies;
- optimized for affordability without unacceptable overcrowding.

### 5.2 Maskan Comfort

Target: young employees and professionals.

- mix of private and shared rooms;
- improved furniture and finishes;
- stronger privacy;
- more complete service package;
- mid-market pricing.

### 5.3 Maskan Plus

Target: professionals and potentially expatriates / higher-budget residents.

- private-heavy or potentially private-only inventory;
- higher-quality furniture and finishes;
- stronger privacy and service standards;
- premium locations where economics support the model;
- higher price point.

### Segmentation principle

Do not mix radically different customer propositions in the same apartment. A unit should have a coherent Maskan tier and resident experience.

### Rental model is separate from product tier

`Essential`, `Comfort`, and `Plus` describe the operating standard, furnishing, service level, and price position. They do not describe whether inventory is shared or rented in full.

- **Shared housing:** bookable inventory may be a private room, shared room, or individual bed. Pricing is shown per bookable resident space.
- **Family apartment:** the complete apartment is one bookable inventory unit for one family under one contract. Pricing is shown for the whole apartment per month.
- Family apartments must never be presented as shared inventory or priced as a bed.
- Shared-housing and family-apartment prices must not be compared without accounting for their different price bases.

## 6. Stay, Pricing and Deposit Logic

### Minimum stay

The intended minimum stay can be **one month**.

### Duration pricing

Pricing can improve with longer commitments. A future pricing structure may include:

- 1 month — highest monthly rate;
- 3 months — discounted monthly rate;
- 6 months — further discount;
- 12 months — strongest long-stay rate.

The exact percentages remain a business decision and should not be hard-coded as final assumptions.

### Price basis

Every displayed price must explicitly state its basis:

- shared housing: per resident space / room / bed, as applicable;
- family apartment: the entire apartment per month.

The selected price basis must be snapshotted with the booking and contract so later price changes cannot alter an existing agreement.

### Security deposit

A security deposit is essential. Its value and rules may vary by duration/product. Deposit terms must be stated clearly in the contract.

**Accounting principle:** a deposit is not rental revenue. It remains a liability until refunded or legitimately settled according to the contract.

### Utilities

Utility treatment varies by tier. Higher-tier units may include utilities fully. Lower tiers can use a fair-usage policy to control abnormal consumption.

## 8. Property Acquisition Criteria

An attractive-looking apartment is not automatically a good Maskan property. **Unit economics determine acceptance.**

Initial target profile discussed:

- unfurnished;
- approximately 180–250 m² where appropriate;
- 3+ original bedrooms;
- large reception with useful layout potential;
- at least 2 bathrooms;
- usable kitchen;
- elevator where floor/location makes it necessary;
- reliable internet availability;
- access to transport, universities, offices, or other demand generators;
- approximately 6–9 residents depending on product tier and layout;
- safe ventilation and acceptable resident density.

### Future Maskan Property Score

Potential scoring factors:

- monthly rent / m²;
- rent / sellable bed;
- fit-out cost;
- fit-out / bed;
- expected average revenue per bed;
- break-even occupancy;
- nearby demand generators;
- transport accessibility;
- bathrooms per resident;
- kitchen quality;
- ventilation/natural light;
- partition potential;
- legal suitability;
- internet availability;
- building condition/security;
- elevator;
- expected maintenance burden.

A property with excellent design but weak economics should be rejected.

## 9. Legal and Safety Principles

Each apartment must undergo legal and technical due diligence before onboarding.

Key principles:

- the owner/lease arrangement must explicitly permit the intended Maskan operating model where required;
- do not assume an ordinary residential lease automatically permits subletting or the planned occupancy model;
- partitions must be reviewed for technical, ventilation, fire-safety, building, and legal implications;
- resident contracts must define rights, obligations, payment, deposit, house rules, termination, damage, and other relevant terms;
- CCTV, where used, is restricted to suitable entrances/common spaces and never private areas;
- residents may access only approved common-area cameras for their own active unit;
- Unit Representatives may receive additional common-area views for the same unit, without access to private areas or unrelated units;
- staff and area managers are limited to cameras for their assigned operational area, while portfolio-wide access is reserved for explicitly authorized administrators;
- camera access must be logged, revocable, and enforced server-side; visibility in the interface is not an authorization control;
- identity and contract documents are private data and must be stored securely.

Final legal documents require review by qualified Egyptian legal counsel. The POC does not represent legal approval of any specific operating arrangement.

## 11. Operations Model

The application is intended to become the primary operational layer for resident interaction and management.

Residents should be able to handle:

- booking/stay information;
- payment status;
- deposit information;
- contract access;
- maintenance requests;
- cleaning information;
- renewal;
- support;
- a private conversation with Maskan support for questions and complaints;
- a residence-only group chat for daily coordination with residents of the same active unit;
- house/unit information.

### Resident communication

Support chat and unit chat serve different purposes and must remain separate:

- **Private support chat** is between the resident and authorized Maskan operations. It can be used to explain a plumbing, electrical, payment, or service concern.
- **Unit chat** is limited to verified residents linked to the same active unit. It is for shared-space and daily coordination, not payments, identity documents, contracts, or private disputes.
- residents may attach relevant photos or short videos to explain an issue or coordinate around a shared space; upload limits, file validation, access control, retention, and moderation apply;
- an issue that needs a technician must become a maintenance request with a reference, status, and visit window; chat alone is not the system of record for repair work;
- residents of one unit must never see another unit's messages;
- production communication must support moderation, reporting, retention rules, and auditable role-based access.

Camera access is part of the resident stay and operations dashboard context. It should not appear as an unrelated top-level product area or separate resident tab.

### Unit Representative

The longest-tenured or suitable resident may optionally become a **Unit Representative (ممثل الوحدة)** for very limited coordination.

Allowed examples:

- confirm cleaning completion;
- report common-area issues;
- communicate urgent unit-level information.

Not allowed:

- collecting money;
- imposing penalties;
- changing contracts;
- deciding resident disputes;
- exercising landlord/management authority.

The Unit Representative is not an admin or supervisor.

### Area operations

As density increases, Maskan can create geographic operating clusters.

Illustrative scaling logic:

- 1–5 units: centralized management;
- 6–15 units in a cluster: field coordinator;
- 15–30 units: small area office may become justified;
- 30+ units: dedicated local operations team depending on economics.

These are planning guidelines, not fixed thresholds.

### Cluster strategy

Expansion should favor geographic density within each Egyptian city. For example, several units within one compact city cluster are operationally preferable to the same number scattered across distant locations. Clustering improves cleaning, maintenance, inspection, logistics, and staff utilization while the company remains nationally positioned.

## 13. Operating Models

### Model A — Master Lease

Maskan leases the property, prepares it, operates it, and bears vacancy/operating risk. Maskan retains the upside after rent and operating costs.

### Model B — Maskan Managed (future)

The owner contributes the apartment while Maskan handles preparation/management/marketing under a management fee or revenue-share structure.

This model could reduce capital intensity during expansion.

Previous exploratory listing research showed that organized/shared housing already exists at multiple price points. Examples observed during research included shared beds/rooms in Nasr City, Heliopolis, New Cairo, and organized student accommodation near BUE in Shorouk.

## 16. Unit Economics Framework

Every potential Maskan property should be modeled before signing.

### Revenue

`Monthly Revenue = Sum of realized room/bed prices + realized whole-unit family-apartment prices`

### Direct property contribution

`Property Contribution = Revenue - Property Rent - Direct Operating Costs`

Direct costs can include:

- master rent;
- utilities paid by Maskan;
- cleaning;
- internet;
- routine maintenance reserve;
- consumables;
- property-level operational costs.

Central staff, taxes, depreciation, financing, and other corporate overhead should be modeled separately where appropriate.

### Critical KPIs

- occupancy rate;
- revenue per available bed;
- average realized monthly revenue per occupied bed;
- break-even occupancy;
- property contribution margin;
- fit-out / bed;
- acquisition cost per resident;
- average stay duration;
- renewal rate;
- maintenance cost / resident or property;
- collection / overdue-payment rate.

### Scenario modeling

Financial planning should include at least:

- 70% occupancy;
- 85% occupancy;
- 95% occupancy;

and Conservative / Base / Optimistic assumptions.

A stabilized occupancy around 85–90%+ would generally make the concept more attractive, but no occupancy target should substitute for property-specific economics.

### Illustrative examples from concept work

Prior exploratory scenarios included a Nasr City unit where high rent and conservative resident pricing produced weak economics, demonstrating why property selection is critical. A better Nasr City scenario and a Shorouk scenario produced positive property-level contribution before central overhead. These were **illustrative calculations only**, not actual Maskan operating results.

## 19. Brand and Experience Principles

Maskan should feel organized, trustworthy, modern, and residential — not like a crowded hostel and not like a generic classifieds website.

Customer-facing principles:

- every flow should reinforce the three primary promises: Speed, Trust, and Safety;
- clear pricing and accountable service are essential evidence of Trust;
- verified identities, controlled access, and documented records are essential evidence of Safety;
- simple language;
- Arabic-first experience using natural Egyptian wording, with English available as an account preference;
- nationwide Egyptian positioning; do not present Maskan as a Cairo-only company;
- transparent pricing;
- excellent apartment photography;
- minimal visual clutter;
- clear availability;
- obvious inclusions/services;
- confidence in management and support;
- clear separation between Essential, Comfort, and Plus.

The property itself should be the visual hero.

## 22. Core Business Rules for Product Development

When implementing software, preserve these rules unless the business reference is deliberately updated:

1. Maskan is an operator, not merely a listing marketplace.
2. Inventory hierarchy is Property → Room → Bed.
3. Even a private room can be represented by one bed/inventory slot internally.
4. Male/female shared units are separate.
5. A property has one coherent tier: Essential, Comfort, or Plus.
6. A room may be original or partitioned.
7. Booking duration can affect price and deposit.
8. Deposits are tracked separately from revenue/rent payments.
9. Residents must only access their own private records.
10. Identity documents, contracts, and maintenance evidence require private storage/access controls.
11. Maintenance has a trackable workflow, not merely a contact form.
12. Unit Representatives have limited coordination privileges and are not admins.
13. Demo financial data must be identifiable as illustrative, not actual performance.
14. Property acceptance should eventually be driven by economics and operational/legal suitability.
15. The system should remain capable of supporting geographic operational clusters.
16. Speed: customer journeys should minimize steps and time from registration to move-in.
17. Trust: pricing, deposits, included services, and the responsible Maskan team must be clear before commitment.
18. Safety: identity, contracts, payments, deposits, and support activity must be documented and access-controlled.
19. Maskan is positioned for Egypt as a whole; pilot clusters do not limit the brand to one city.
20. Rental model and product tier are separate dimensions.
21. A family apartment is one whole-unit booking, and its displayed monthly price covers the complete apartment.
22. The current demo dataset is limited to Greater Cairo while the target market remains Egypt-wide.
